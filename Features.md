# Unique Selling Propositions (USPs)

## USP 1. Constraint-Aware Multi-Vehicle Route Optimization

Generates feasible delivery schedules by considering vehicle type, vehicle capacity, and customer delivery time windows instead of optimizing solely for the shortest path.

## USP 2. Automatic Neighborhood-Based Order Batching & Sequencing

Automatically clusters nearby deliveries and determines an optimized delivery sequence to minimize travel distance and improve fleet utilization.

## USP 3. Dynamic Event-Driven Route Re-optimization

Continuously adapts delivery schedules in response to operational events such as urgent order insertions, driver delays, delivery cancellations, and simulated traffic disruptions.

## USP 4. Integrated Dispatcher–Driver Platform

Provides a unified ecosystem comprising a dispatcher dashboard and a driver mobile application, enabling seamless coordination from route planning to delivery execution, proof of delivery, exception reporting, and workforce management.

## USP 5. Regional Language Voice Assistant

Enables drivers to interact with the application through voice commands in regional languages for navigation within the app, task access, exception reporting, and other routine operations, improving accessibility and reducing manual interaction.
